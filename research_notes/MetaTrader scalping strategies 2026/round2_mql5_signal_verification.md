## Key Question 1: Which MQL5 signals satisfy all four criteria (3+ years, max DD <= ~30 %, positive lifetime growth, scalping-style), and how many out of the universe?

### Takeaway
Of ~4,275 ranked signals (1,608 MT4 over 34 pages, 2,667 MT5 over 56 pages), 378 have 156+ weeks of history, 332 of those are positive, 90 also show a list drawdown <= 30 % (62 MT4 / 28 MT5), and only 15 of the 90 hold trades for <= 4 hours on average (0.35 % of the universe); a further 17 trade 25+ times per week but hold for 6 hours to 21 days, which is the signature of grid/basket systems rather than scalping. Only 7 signals combine <= 4-hour holds with >= 10 trades/week, and three of the 15 short-hold survivors are one vendor's news-spike accounts whose 9,000-45,000 % growth figures sit on USD 1,000 deposits with zero subscribers.
