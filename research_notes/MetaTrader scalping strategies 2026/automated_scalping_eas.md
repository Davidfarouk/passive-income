Research date: 2026-11-05. Method: 20 web searches (budget then exhausted) and ~40 page fetches, including MQL5 Market product pages, MQL5 Signals pages, MQL5 seller profiles, vendor sites, Trustpilot, GitHub READMEs, and third-party review blogs. Myfxbook, ForexPeaceArmy, Forex Factory and Reddit all returned HTTP 403 / were blocked from this environment, so their data is reported only second-hand (flagged) or listed as a gap.

Source-quality legend used throughout: **[VENDOR]** = developer's own claim; **[RESELLER/AFFILIATE]** = commercial review site or reseller; **[MQL5 SIGNAL]** = broker-fed statistics on mql5.com (verifiable but note that the signal page did not expose Real/Demo status to the fetcher); **[USER REVIEW]** = individual buyer report; **[INDEPENDENT]** = no apparent commercial tie.

## Key Question 1: Which scalping EAs are top sellers / top rated on the MQL5 Market in 2025-2026, and what do descriptions and reviews say about method, drawdown and real-account results?

### Takeaway
The MQL5 Market's best-selling "scalper" category in 2025-2026 is dominated by gold (XAUUSD) robots, and a large share of the highest-rated ones (the Quantum series, Waka Waka, Perceptrader AI, Golden Pickaxe, Tokyo EA, Bonnitta) are grid / averaging / recovery systems rather than single-entry scalpers. The products with explicit single-entry, fixed-stop designs (Gold Reaper, Gold Trade Pro, Night Hunter Pro, Smart Gold Hunter) carry noticeably lower ratings (4.0-4.4) and more polarized reviews, because their losses are visible rather than hidden in floating drawdown.

### Inferences
- Rating and sales leadership on MQL5 is inversely related to transparency of losses: grid/recovery products accumulate near-perfect ratings while their drawdowns live in floating P&L and periodic blow-ups (Quantum Emperor June 2024; Quantum Valkyrie March 2026), whereas fixed-stop breakout/scalper products (Gold Reaper 4.4, Gold Trade Pro 4.35, Smart Gold Hunter 4.01) show their losses as closed trades and are rated lower.
- Vendors re-list "X"/"new" versions (Quantum Queen → Quantum Queen X; Gold Reaper → "Gold Reaper New V2"), which resets review counts and signal histories; the original Quantum Queen's 4.96-4.98 rating vs. Quantum Queen X's 4.08 suggests the hype does not transfer.
- Products explicitly marketed as "scalpers" on MQL5 in 2026 are overwhelmingly XAUUSD M1-M15 systems; classic EUR/GBP major-pair scalpers (Dark Algo, PipHunter) are few and low-volume.

### Gaps
- MQL5 does not publish a sales-ranked list to anonymous fetchers; "purchases per month" was only visible on individual product pages (Gold Reaper 112/mo, Gold Trade Pro 7/mo). No official 2025-2026 best-seller ranking was obtained.
- The Quantum Emperor MT4/MT5 product pages and the Puscasu seller page could not be fetched directly (redirect / 404 on guessed handle), so the official live-signal ID and current review text are unverified.
- "AI Gen XII" and "Zeus / Smart Scalper" variants: search budget was exhausted before these could be researched; no data.
- Review text on MQL5 for Quantum Queen, Perceptrader AI (MT4 page shows "No reviews"), Bonnitta and Aura Ultimate was not retrieved.

## Key Question 5: Status of latency / tick-arbitrage scalping EAs with brokers in 2026

### Takeaway
By 2026 latency arbitrage is treated as "toxic flow" across retail brokers and prop firms: sources agree the standard response sequence is spread widening → execution delay/last look → trade cancellation and account closure with profit withheld, and prop firms add 30-60-second minimum hold rules and immediate termination for latency arbitrage. The leading arbitrage-software vendor itself now says the method works only "where the broker is slower than you and costs leave something," with its own tick recordings showing the edge collapsing from +11.7 points at 30 ms to +0.2 points at 100 ms.

### Cited Findings
- LiquidityFinder (CEO Sam Low; published Jul 2023, updated Jul 8 2026): brokers use analytics from at least nine third-party providers; first step is to widen spreads shown to that trader, then "withhold any profits made and take action to shut the account," often within a few trades; some brokers add execution delays or reject fast orders; traders targeting the 22:00 London rollover (when some broker prices disappear for seconds or minutes) "were identified and their accounts closed"; the practice "has now declined significantly"; advice: "change your strategy!" — [LiquidityFinder](https://liquidityfinder.com/insight/other/latency-arbitragein-forex-trading-easy-profits-not-really)
- Westernpips (arbitrage software vendor, Oct 7 2026 / updated Nov 2 2026): acknowledges countermeasures — rejecting/repricing stale orders, minimum holding time, spread widening, trade cancellation/account closure where the client agreement allows, last look; detection via markouts, very short holds, high win rates on short trades, timing at price spikes, linked accounts; its own gold recording (May 26 2026) shows mean gross +11.7 points per signal at 30 ms falling to about +0.2 points at 100 ms; "Private 7" lifetime licence "from US$777"; "many [prop firms] prohibit" the strategy — [Westernpips](https://westernpips.com/latency-arbitrage.html)
- Prop-firm detection (Apr 7 2026, updated Aug 8 2026): order-to-fill ratios above ~50:1 or 100:1, fills compared against institutional feeds (LMAX, CQG, Global Prime), fixed trade durations, zero negative slippage over 100 trades, order layering, journal "Order Modify" spam; FTMO and Alpha Capital Group cited as running millisecond-level risk engines; FundedNext and Maven Trading described as using behavioral profiling; consequence is manual review then termination or payout denial — [PropFirmScan](https://propfirmscan.com/blog/prop-firm-hft-fingerprinting-why-your-latency-arbitrage-fails)
- Prop-firm rule summary (updated Aug 29 2026): latency arbitrage is "the most aggressively monitored technical violation"; many firms exclude trades held under 30 or 60 seconds from profit calculations (example: a +2 pip scalp closed in 12 seconds deducted on audit); exponential lot scaling (1.0 → 2.0 → 4.0) "strictly prohibited" by top-tier firms, fixed-lot grids with stops sometimes allowed; "high-frequency order spam" prohibited; hard violations → account terminated and fees forfeited — [PropFirmPayouts](https://propfirmpayouts.io/guides/banned-prop-firm-strategies-arbitrage-grid-ea-rules)
- A VPS vendor's guide (May 2025, updated Dec 23 2025) says arbitrage is "still profitable — but only if you have the infrastructure to compete"; lists Tickmill and RoboForex as brokers whose terms permit arbitrage and IC Markets as "unclear"; quotes software costs of Westernpips $2,500-$3,750/month (conflicting with Westernpips' own "$777 lifetime"), SharpTrader $3,494-$6,294, HFT Arbitrage Platform $2,605; claims 95% of arbitrage gaps last ≤5 seconds and 60% ≤1 second (unsourced) — [NYC Servers [AFFILIATE]](https://newyorkcityservers.com/blog/is-forex-arbitrage-still-profitable-in-2025-strategies-that-still-work)
- Prop-firm guide notes brokers may terminate a prop firm's own corporate account if it passes too much toxic flow upstream — [PropFirmScan](https://propfirmscan.com/blog/prop-firm-hft-fingerprinting-why-your-latency-arbitrage-fails)
- MQL5 Market still lists an "Infinity Pips HFT" XAUUSD EA at $30,000 with no rating — [MQL5 MT4 listing](https://www.mql5.com/en/market/mt4)

### Inferences
- No regulator has banned latency arbitrage; enforcement is contractual (broker T&Cs, prop-firm rules) and technological (last look, delays, analytics), which is why vendors can still legally sell the software while most users' accounts get closed.
- The only brokers named as tolerant (Tickmill, RoboForex) come from an affiliate page and should be re-verified against current terms; LiquidityFinder's statement that it only onboards traders who can prove non-toxic flow ("a very rare thing") is the stronger signal of industry stance.

### Gaps
- No documented 2025-2026 enforcement case with names, dates and amounts was found; all account-closure reports are generic. No data on how many accounts are closed per year for arbitrage.
- Tick-arbitrage between two retail brokers (2-leg "lock" arbitrage) was not specifically researched.

- Treating one vendor's signal roster as a sample, roughly a third of long-running strategies from a top-rated developer are flat or busted after 3-9 years, and the survivors compound at ~0.6-1.7%/month; this is the best available proxy for a base rate and it excludes products that were delisted (Evening Scalper Pro) or whose signals were deleted (Bonnitta 669290, Golden Pickaxe 1693250), so the true survival rate is lower.
- Review-site rankings and MQL5 "reliability" sorts systematically remove failures, so any list of "best EAs" overstates the base rate.
