## KQ1. Documented cases (2023-2026) of near-100%-win-rate EAs / signals that blew up, were discontinued, or drew regulatory action

### Takeaway
No regulator in 2023-2026 has sanctioned a *named MetaTrader scalping EA* as such; enforcement has instead hit (a) "automated trading robot"/"AI bot" investment schemes that never traded (CFTC, SEC, BaFin, ASIC), (b) forex-training MLMs (FTC v. IM Mastery Academy), and (c) unauthorised social-media promoters of forex CFDs (FCA, ASIC). The best-documented *mechanical* blow-ups of high-win-rate systems come from public MQL5 Signals and Myfxbook pages (e.g., the "MagicGW audcad L" MT5 signal, 79.5% win rate, 709% "growth", equity 97% below balance, subscriptions suspended by MQL5 in 2026) and from grid EAs such as Waka Waka (one Myfxbook account went from a $6,021 peak to $0.00 in Feb 2022).

### Inferences
- The enforcement record shows a consistent pattern: the "100% win / guaranteed" label is used both by (i) outright Ponzi/no-trading schemes (ROFX, MTI, EmpiresX, TSAI, Cryptoaiml) and (ii) real EAs/signals whose win rate is manufactured by martingale/grid/no-stop-loss logic (MagicGW, Waka Waka). Regulators pursue (i); (ii) is left to platform warnings (MQL5's "Subscription not permitted") and buyer caveats.
- The MagicGW timeline (profitable Nov-Dec 2025 reviews → Nov 2025/Jan 2026 record drawdowns → provider top-ups → "BLEW ACCOUNT" Oct 2026) is a textbook illustration of a ~80% win-rate martingale signal surviving ~10-20 months before ruin, with balance top-ups used to mask drawdown statistics.
- Vendor rotation and product removal (MQL5 rules permit removal "without any explanation") mean discontinued EAs leave little public trail, which explains why so few named 2023-2026 EA blow-ups are documented outside forums.

### Gaps
- No regulator action in 2023-2026 names a specific MT4/MT5 *scalping EA* product; enforcement targets are schemes, promoters and MLMs. Named-EA failures rest on platform pages and review sites, not adjudicated findings.
- ForexPeaceArmy "scam alert" pages, ForexFactory threads ("Made a 100% Winrate EA", "Very simple mechanical system with 100% win rate"), BabyPips guide and Myfxbook FAQ all returned HTTP 403; Reddit post-mortems did not surface in search. Searches for specific EAs (Quantum Emperor, Golden Pickaxe, Forex Fury demo controversy) and for the Oct-2025 gold-crash impact on grid EAs could not be run (search budget exhausted).
- No aggregate dollar loss figure exists for any single EA; subscriber losses on MagicGW are individual claims ($480, $596, "1/3 of my account").
- The 2024 FCA case against nine finfluencers (Emmanuel Nwanze et al.) was not retrievable for this note.

## KQ2. Why a martingale / grid / no-stop-loss scalper can show 95-100% wins for months and then lose everything

### Takeaway
A doubling martingale with a leg cap L wins 1 - (1/2)^L of its *baskets* but loses 2^L - 1 units when it fails, so the expected value before costs is exactly zero and negative after spreads/swaps; the "win rate" is a property of the sizing rule, not of any edge. Published simulations show 94-100% basket win rates coexisting with net losses in 13 of 16 settings, and gambler's-ruin theory makes eventual ruin certain for any finite account.

### Cited Findings
- Doubling identity and failure odds: in a standard martingale the whole position "always recovers within one stop distance of the last entry" (2^n = 2^0 + 2^1 + ... + 2^(n-1) + 1). With leg limit L and 50:50 trades a sequence fails with probability (1/2)^L; a failed sequence loses 2^L - 1 units vs 1 unit per win; "Before costs, the net result is zero. After costs, it is negative." "This is the martingale dilemma: the higher the limit, the rarer the loss, but the bigger it is." "martingale can't improve your odds of winning. It just postpones your losses." — [Forexop, Martingale Strategy in Forex (updated 1 Oct 2026)](https://forexop.com/martingale-trading-system-overview/)
- Leg-limit figures: 8 legs → 98% chance of at least one wipe-out within 1,000 sequences, max position 128x first trade; 12 legs → 22% chance, but max position 2,048x and one failure costs 4,095x a normal win. Rule of thumb: max lots = 2^(legs-1) x first lot; max loss ≈ (2^legs - 1) x step. Example: 0.01 lot, 40-pip step, 8 legs → 1.28 lots max, ≈ $1,020 failure cost on EUR/USD about once every 256 sequences. — [Forexop](https://forexop.com/martingale-trading-system-overview/)
- Backtest (EUR/USD, GBP/USD, AUD/USD, USD/JPY; ~17,000 hourly bars each; Dec 2023-Sep 2026): "13 of 16 settings (4, 6, 8, 10 legs x 4 pairs) lost money. All settings won 94-100% of baskets." EUR/USD 10 legs: 99.8% of baskets won, equity +~$2,600 by early 2025, then "One failed basket cost $2,916"; account -$315 by Sep 2026. USD/JPY 10-leg: +$6,545 in main test but -$9,811 with hourly-close modelling. Largest EUR/USD position 5.12 lots (~$580,000 notional) needing ~$19,400 margin at 1:30. Swaps excluded, which "flatters the results". — [Forexop](https://forexop.com/martingale-trading-system-overview/)
- Trend risk from daily closes since 2004: EUR/USD had "99 one-way moves needing more than 8 doublings (about four a year) and 50 needing more than 10"; the largest (Dec 2008) "ran 2,319 pips without a 90-pip pullback"; "intraday swings are larger". — [Forexop](https://forexop.com/martingale-trading-system-overview/)
- Cycle arithmetic (10 USD base, 6-leg cap, 50% loss odds): six straight losses = 0.5^6 = 1/64 ≈ 1.5625%; realised loss after six losses 630 USD, next stake 640 USD; "One failed cycle cancels 63 successful cycles before costs"; over 100 funded cycles P(at least one failure) = 1 - (63/64)^100 ≈ 79.3%. Grid variant: aggregate volume after n additions = q x (2^(n+1) - 1). "Long backtests can omit unfinished baskets, adverse paths, and fill or funding constraints." — [PineConnector, Martingale Strategy Risk](https://www.pineconnector.com/blogs/pico-blog/martingale-grid-risk-of-ruin)
- Capital does not buy safety: on a 20-pip EURUSD grid, 2.0x multiplier, 0.01 base lot, 1:100 leverage, a $1,000 account dies at step 6 (100-pip runway), $10,000 at step 9 (160 pips), $100,000 at step 12 (220 pips): "increasing your capital by a factor of 10x only buys you roughly 3 additional grid steps." Gold: a $10,000 account "hits liquidation in just 5 steps (80 pips / $8.00 price move)." Step 8 = 1.28-lot tranche, 2.55 lots cumulative, "255x original base exposure". Prop-firm $100k account with 5% daily limit and 0.10 base lot "fails by Step 4 or 5, after a tiny 75-pip market move." "Every dollar earned by a Martingale EA across hundreds of winning days is simply a temporary loan borrowed from the single, inevitable liquidation event." (Broker-published; no run count given.) — [FXNX, Martingale Survival Test (23 Aug 2026)](https://fxnx.com/en/blog/martingale-survival-test-measured-runs-account-failure)
- Gambler's ruin: a gambler with finite wealth playing a fair game "will eventually and inevitably go broke against an opponent with infinite wealth"; ruin probability "tends to 1 even in the scenario where the game is fair". Unfair-game formula: P1 = (1 - (p/q)^n2) / (1 - (p/q)^(n1+n2)). — [Wikipedia, Gambler's ruin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gambler%27s_ruin)
- Taleb distribution: "a high probability of a small gain, and a small probability of a very large loss, which more than outweighs the gains"; "picking up pennies in front of a steamroller" (Taleb, p.19); "camouflaged by the appearance of low risk and steady returns"; carries "a risk of ruin that approaches eventual certainty over time"; numerical case: "$1 gain 99.9% of the time and a $500 loss 0.1% of the time" has positive EV, roughly zero at 0.2%, negative at 0.3% — i.e., profitability hinges on a tail probability that cannot be estimated from a short record; "because extreme events are rare, they have often not been observed yet". — [Wikipedia, Taleb distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taleb_distribution)
- Short-vol analogy (XIV, 2018): such strategies "often work for long periods of time, and the longer they work, the safer they seem and the more money floods into them, until the steamroller obliterates everyone." — [Livewire / Forager](https://www.livewiremarkets.com/wires/a-new-generation-learns-about-nickels-and-steamrollers)
- Win rate without R:R is meaningless: a provider boasting "a 90% win rate" with 10-pip wins vs 150-pip stops nets 900 pips won, 1,500 lost, "a net loss of 600 pips" per 100 trades. — [FXNX, Telegram signal check (7 Oct 2026)](https://fxnx.com/en/blog/how-check-telegram-forex-signal-group-before-you-pay)
- Holding losers manufactures the ratio: "Unscrupulous signal sellers maintain an artificial 95%+ win rate by simply holding losing positions indefinitely"; closed balance "shows a 100% win rate, but their floating equity is sitting at a -45% drawdown canyon"; doubling 0.01→0.02→0.04→0.08 lots then "a further 80-pip drop triggers a full margin blowout". — [FXNX, Verify a Track Record (29 Aug 2026)](https://fxnx.com/en/blog/forex-signal-providers-verify-track-record-before-you-copy)
- Copy-trading asymmetry: "the Signals Provider may just wait out temporary drawdown having a large volume order, while the Subscriber may lose all the funds with all his or her positions closed by Stop Out." — [MQL5 Articles 618, General information on Trading Signals](https://www.mql5.com/en/articles/618)
- Grid trading in gold: "gold's frequent news-driven runs of 500 to 1,000 pips without retracing can exponentially increase grid drawdown"; a reviewed gold grid EA's "maximum equity drawdown has reached over 65%". — [search summaries: FXNX prop-ready gold EA](https://fxnx.com/en/blog/build-prop-ready-gold-ea-mql5-surviving-xauusd-volatility), [ForexRobotLab ABS GoldGrid review](https://forexrobotlab.com/abs-goldgrid-ea-review/)

### Inferences
- A 99%+ basket win rate implies a leg cap of roughly 7-10 doublings; at that depth a single adverse run of ~200-250 pips (which EUR/USD has produced ~4 times a year since 2004 on daily closes alone, more often intraday) consumes the entire account on typical retail leverage. Months of smooth equity are therefore the *expected* observation before ruin, not evidence against it.
- Because the loss is a single cliff event, standard metrics computed on closed trades (win rate, profit factor, Sharpe on balance) are uninformative; only equity-based drawdown and the maximum lot multiple reveal the risk.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed study specific to retail forex martingale EAs was found; the quantitative references are practitioner blogs (Forexop, PineConnector, FXNX — the latter a CFD broker with a commercial interest) and general probability theory.
- The FXNX survival test gives no run count or data period, so its step-of-death figures are illustrative, not reproducible.

## KQ3. How fake track records are produced, and the tells

### Takeaway
Fake or misleading records are produced through five main routes — demo accounts shown as "real" or run on private MT4 servers, Myfxbook accounts with hidden history/open trades/deposits, backtests run on MT4's 90%-modelling-quality synthetic ticks with fixed spread and zero slippage, survivorship (publish the one account out of many that survived), and closed-trade "balance" statistics that hide floating losses. The tells are correspondingly: missing verification badges, "Other (MT4)" server, equity far below balance, hidden lot sizes, gain not matching absolute gain, and backtests that stop at the EA's last upload date.

### Cited Findings

**Demo-as-live and private servers**
- A tester set an FxOpen *demo* account to "real" and after full verification "Myfxbook shows demo account as a real account after a full verification process"; the only trace was the settings window showing "the FxOpen demo server". (Myfxbook later fixed the display.) — [EA-Coder, Fake Myfxbook Account (2017)](https://www.ea-coder.com/fake-myfxbook-account/)
- "One of the worst things for an account to have is to be listed as 'Other (MT4)'" because "too many brokers these days are fake and can easily provide fictitious trading history and results"; trading privileges are "verified when the trader opens a pending order with a key given by Myfxbook". — [AtoZ Markets, How to Identify Fake Myfxbook Accounts (3 Mar 2022)](https://atozmarkets.com/guides/how-to-identify-fake-myfxbook-accounts/)
- On private servers "the administrator can manipulate historical pricing, erase losing orders retroactively"; read-only investor feeds from someone else's profitable account can be resold by "an unscrupulous marketer"; if the broker is unregulated, "treat the entire track record as fabricated." Both Myfxbook badges required: "Track Record Verified" (platform connects to broker history) and "Trading Privileges Verified" (publisher controls the account); "If either is missing, audit fails." — [FXNX, Verify a Track Record](https://fxnx.com/en/blog/forex-signal-providers-verify-track-record-before-you-copy)
- CFTC: in the MTI scheme "fake customer accounts and balances were created using MetaTrader demo accounts." — [CFTC Release 8854-24](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/8854-24)
- "Demo accounts incur zero real-world slippage, zero liquidity rejections, and no overnight swap stress." — [FXNX](https://fxnx.com/en/blog/forex-signal-providers-verify-track-record-before-you-copy); demos "don't pay slippage" — [ForexCracked 12-point checklist (13 May 2026)](https://www.forexcracked.com/forex-ea/how-to-spot-forex-ea-scams-checklist/)

**Hidden history, hidden deposits, gain manipulation**
- Hidden data: a hidden trading history can hide "overleveraging, holding onto losing trades for 1000s of pips, doubling, quadrupling up on trades"; a missing deposits/withdrawals record can conceal "rescue deposits or account resets". — [search summaries of BabyPips guide and SmartEdge](https://forums.babypips.com/t/a-guide-to-using-and-recognising-myfxbook-accounts/52656); "Deposits/withdrawals are hidden: you cannot see rescue deposits or resets"; "Open trades are private: you cannot see floating drawdowns"; "Many aggressive EAs show equity drawdowns of 60% to 80% or more." — [SmartEdge Trading (updated 2025)](https://smartedgetrading.net/blog/how-to-read-myfxbook-and-ea-track-records)
- Gain inflation: "both 'Gain' and 'Absolute gain' can be manipulated, especially the gain, and are affected by deposits" — a $1 deposit plus a $100 top-up can show "10000%" gain; "The loosing trade will not appear on his Myfxbook account until it will be closed." — [AtoZ Markets](https://atozmarkets.com/guides/how-to-identify-fake-myfxbook-accounts/); an account showing "more than 53,000%" gain with balance, equity, lots and profits hidden; martingale account with lot size "set to private"; "Scammers might grow a small account by 1000% and then deposit extra $10,000 USD." — [EA-Coder](https://www.ea-coder.com/fake-myfxbook-account/)
- Balance top-ups to mask drawdown in live signals: MQL5 subscribers alleged "the provider doubled the account balance, which may have reduced the apparent drawdown" — the page shows relative drawdown 77.11% by balance vs 96.93% by equity. — [MQL5 Signals 2280569](https://www.mql5.com/en/signals/2280569)
- Survivorship: a provider "opens many accounts and deletes the 18 blown accounts", keeping survivors. — [FXNX](https://fxnx.com/en/blog/forex-signal-providers-verify-track-record-before-you-copy); "Vendors may set accounts to private after drawdowns"; "Vendor rotation: scammers relaunch the same EA under new names"; "Review rings: interlinked review sites with shared operators"; "Backtest date cherry-picking: backtests that skip stressful periods". — [ForexCracked](https://www.forexcracked.com/forex-ea/how-to-spot-forex-ea-scams-checklist/)
- Screenshots: "Non-clickable screenshots with no links are nothing but fabricated images." — [EA-Coder](https://www.ea-coder.com/fake-myfxbook-account/); "Never trust a screenshot of a blue MetaTrader profit screen" — balances can be altered with browser "Inspect Element" on a web terminal; watch for "edited" tags on signals where a stop-loss was changed after a loss, "ghost entries" (three conflicting pending orders posted, wrong ones deleted), and "Take Profit" replies to a "Message deleted". — [FXNX, Telegram check](https://fxnx.com/en/blog/how-check-telegram-forex-signal-group-before-you-pay)
- Deepfakes/AI: AI can generate "dynamic, real-time videos of trading terminals showing millions of dollars in fabricated assets"; "AI persona swarms" simulate winning communities. — [ACY Partners (updated 30 Jul 2026)](https://www.acypartners.com/blog/what-is-forex-affiliate-telegram-scams-with-ai-exposed)

**Backtest artefacts (MT4 Strategy Tester)**
- "The MT4 Strategy Tester is mathematically capped at 90% modeling quality when using standard historical data"; "Every Tick" mode generates synthetic ticks from M1 OHLC bars in "a predictable sequence of artificial ticks"; default fixed "Current" spread is "largely fictional"; spreads "can spike 500% or more during the Asian rollover window"; the tester assumes orders fill "instantly, with zero network delay and perfect price matching"; requotes "MT4's tester never models them"; "Retail traders typically face latencies of 50-300 ms"; strategies relying on trades under 5 pips "are likely to fail live due to latency"; "It is possible to inadvertently code an EA that 'exploits' the predictable fractal algorithm"; look-ahead bias via iClose(...,0) on an incomplete bar. Fix: real tick data (99%+), walk-forward, multi-broker, spread stress test ("Run the test at 5 pips spread; if the strategy dies, it's not robust"), ~3 months forward test. (Vendor site; some statistics unsourced.) — [MT4 Programming, Beyond the 90% Myth (updated 17 May 2026)](https://mt4programming.com/beyond-the-90-myth-why-mt4-strategy-tester-results-fail-in-live-markets-and-how-to-fix-it/)
- Scalping is uniquely sensitive: interpolation "assumes a single price path per candle"; for strategies that depend on intrabar price action "that guesswork can make a losing strategy look profitable"; MT4 "does not simulate slippage by default". — [search summaries: ForexTester, TradersJournal, Pineify](https://forextester.com/blog/mt4-strategy-tester/)
- MQL5 Market rules forbid presenting "trading strategy backtest results as real trading results, whether expressly or implicitly" (IV.3) and promising profits through "Product name, logo, screenshots or description"; yet the Administration "has the right to remove any Product from the Market service without any explanation" (I.8) and "Products purchased through the Market service can not be returned" (IV.14), with refunds only where "the Seller commits fraud or misleads buyers" (VII.6). — [MQL5 Market Rules](https://www.mql5.com/en/market/rules)
- Vendor backtest example (Quantum Queen, gold grid EA, $1,299): 2018-2024 backtest "$10,000 grew to over $21 million, with an 87.85% win rate and 18.64% maximum drawdown"; vendor says v2.0 (May 2025) "removed martingale"; reviewer relied on vendor data and "did not independently confirm live drawdown". — [NYC Servers, Quantum Queen review (28 Nov 2025)](https://newyorkcityservers.com/blog/quantum-queen-ea-review). Another scalping-EA site advertises "a backtest profit of $276,800,229 from a $1,000 deposit". — [search summary](https://forexscalpingea.com/)

**Statistical tells**
- Profit factor "above 3.0 on a large sample size" may signal hidden losses or curve-fitted demo conditions; healthy range 1.50-2.20; fewer than 300 closed trades or less than six months of continuous live data flagged; institutional standard "500+ closed trades" over "12 to 24 months"; test across "Trend + Range + Shock" regimes (central bank decisions, NFP). — [FXNX, Verify a Track Record](https://fxnx.com/en/blog/forex-signal-providers-verify-track-record-before-you-copy)
- "the smoothness of the equity curve in the visible period is what fools people"; "five years of beautiful equity curves can vanish in a single 200-pip move that goes against you on a Sunday open." — [ForexCracked](https://www.forexcracked.com/forex-ea/how-to-spot-forex-ea-scams-checklist/)

### Inferences
- Most of these techniques are not illegal in themselves (Myfxbook privacy settings, demo accounts, MT4 default tester settings), which is why platforms rather than regulators police them and why the burden falls on the buyer to demand equity-based, badge-verified, multi-year records with visible open trades and deposits.
- A record where balance drawdown is small but equity drawdown is large (MagicGW: 77% vs 97%) is the single most reliable quantitative tell of a martingale/averaging system.

### Gaps
- Myfxbook's own FAQ (403) could not be read to confirm current verification mechanics; the EA-Coder demo-as-real finding dates from 2017 and Myfxbook reportedly fixed the display.
- No 2025-2026 documented case of a fake *private MT4 server* being exposed was found; the technique is described by practitioners, not proven in a case.

## KQ5. Red flags experienced traders list for scalping EAs/signals

### Takeaway
Independent checklists converge on a short list of "hard stops": no badge-verified live account (both Myfxbook badges), martingale/grid relabelled as "recovery"/"averaging", hidden open trades or lot sizes, single-broker (especially unregulated, IB-linked) requirements, Telegram-only distribution with crypto-only payment, monthly return claims above ~15%, fewer than ~5 risk settings, and impossible refund conditions.

### Cited Findings
- ForexCracked 12-point checklist (13 May 2026): (1) "If an EA claims 30% per month consistently - or worse, per week - close the tab" (monthly claims above 15% fail); (2) no verified track record = "Hard stop"; (3) developer whose only footprint is their sales page; (4) pricing theatre ("Nobody ever paid $2,997"); (5) "Calling it 'smart recovery' or 'averaging strategy' or 'anti-grid recovery system' doesn't change the math" = "Hard stop"; (6) "AI/neural network" marketing without specifying inputs/retraining/inference location; (7) requests for live account number/investor password; (8) broker lock-in where the developer is an IB who "earns a commission on every trade you place"; (9) fake reviews (reverse-image-search testimonials); (10) "If there are fewer than five risk-related settings, walk away"; (11) "Telegram-only = anonymous distribution = no accountability"; (12) refund clauses such as "void if EA was loaded on a live account". — [ForexCracked](https://www.forexcracked.com/forex-ea/how-to-spot-forex-ea-scams-checklist/)
- NYC Servers 10 red flags (18 Dec 2025): "100% win rate", "Never lose a trade", "Guaranteed 500% monthly returns"; martingale example 0.2 → 0.8 → 3.2 → 12.8 lots (4x per loss); grid anecdote: "I left a Grid EA running overnight on EU and it made about $300. Unfortunately, in the morning London session it hit max drawdown and lost $540"; if "Open Positions" is hidden on Myfxbook that is a warning; valid account should show both badges, ≥6 months live, drawdown under 30%; "Other MT4" server and Gain ≠ Absolute Gain are warnings; "A scam EA is almost always promoted in conjunction with a specific unregulated, offshore broker" (ACY Securities), with affiliate commissions of "20% to 50% of the deposit"; unmeetable refund conditions (trade exactly 0.01 lots, specific pairs, London session, spreads <1.5 pips, logs within 14 days); a Forex Factory user "reported losing $15,000 over five years on EAs promising 'consistent profits'". — [NYC Servers](https://newyorkcityservers.com/blog/how-to-spot-forex-ea-scams)
- Telegram-specific (FXNX, 7 Oct 2026): "Free VIP access" tied to opening an account through the provider's broker link with a $250 deposit is an affiliate funnel; such providers "blast 10 to 15 scalp signals a day" to earn volume commissions; $100/month payable only in USDT/BTC/P2P removes chargebacks; confirm "every single trade has a hard stop-loss attached at the time of entry"; reject advice to "add to the position" or "DCA"; disabled comments and a single anonymous admin are warnings; "the provider's 5-pip gain easily becomes your 2-pip loss" after latency and spread. — [FXNX Telegram check](https://fxnx.com/en/blog/how-check-telegram-forex-signal-group-before-you-pay)
- Affiliate economics (ACY Partners, Jul 2026): the Telegram operator "earns a split of the client's losses, often up to 50-70%" through an unregulated offshore broker, so the signals are "mathematically designed to drain your account balance"; claims of "an impossible 98% trading win rate" and "90%+ win rates"; withdrawal "tax"/"liquidation fee" of 20%; VIP channel with "only a few spaces remain". — [ACY Partners](https://www.acypartners.com/blog/what-is-forex-affiliate-telegram-scams-with-ai-exposed)
- Track-record red flags (FXNX, Aug 2026): balance "never steps down if the trader refuses to close losing positions"; check Open Trades tab for "Duration and Floating P/L" (example: three GBP/USD shorts open four months at -$3,500); micro-account rounding can force "20 times more proportional risk than the master"; <300 trades or <6 months flagged; PF >3.0 suspicious. — [FXNX Verify](https://fxnx.com/en/blog/forex-signal-providers-verify-track-record-before-you-copy)
- SmartEdge: "Lot size increasing sharply after losses" = martingale/uncontrolled grid; "Dozens of small wins followed by a few large losses that erase months of profit"; prefer "at least one serious real account with realistic lot sizes and a normal broker". — [SmartEdge](https://smartedgetrading.net/blog/how-to-read-myfxbook-and-ea-track-records)
- AtoZ Markets: "stop losses are much greater than take profit levels... One lost order has wiped out the profits of 6 months"; "There are a dozen ways to fake an online trade journal account if one really tries hard." — [AtoZ Markets](https://atozmarkets.com/guides/how-to-identify-fake-myfxbook-accounts/)
- Vendor-side admission: a Gold Trap EA listing says "a long, one-directional move can build a large basket and cause a large drawdown or loss of the account"; reviewers say aggressive grid/martingale EAs "will almost certainly violate evaluation rules" at prop firms. — [search summaries](https://algoking.pk/items/gold-trap-ea-latest/1043)
- MQL5 forum: frequent updates "may reflect vendors optimizing parameter sets"; review systems can be gamed; "many customers of these EAs have no knowledge or skill on how to use them properly" (Fernando Carreiro). — [MQL5 forum 457568](https://www.mql5.com/en/forum/457568/page3)
- Regulator red flags: CFTC — "Claims of 100% 'win' rates", pressure from "strangers or influencers online", claims AI can predict markets — [CFTC 8854-24](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/8854-24); ASIC — "guaranteed, quick and easy investment returns", fake "ASIC certified" claims, celebrity deepfakes — [ASIC 26-063MR](https://www.asic.gov.au/about-asic/news-centre/find-a-media-release/2026-releases/26-063mr-asic-ramps-up-action-to-protect-consumers-from-ai-powered-online-investment-scams)

### Inferences
- The IB/affiliate model creates a direct conflict: a signal seller paid on volume or on client losses benefits from over-trading scalp signals and from clients using high leverage at offshore brokers — which is exactly what "10-15 scalp signals a day" and "free VIP for a $250 deposit" produce.
- Note that several checklist publishers (NYC Servers, SmartEdge, FXNX, ACY) sell VPS, EAs or brokerage services; their red-flag lists are nonetheless mutually consistent and consistent with regulator advisories.

### Gaps
- No systematic survey ranking red flags by predictive power was found; all lists are expert opinion.
- Forum sources (ForexFactory, BabyPips, Reddit) with first-hand trader post-mortems were inaccessible or not surfaced.

## KQ6. Copy-trading / social-trading scalping signal outcomes (MQL5 Signals, Myfxbook AutoTrade, ZuluTrade, eToro)

### Takeaway
No platform publishes 2025-2026 subscriber-outcome statistics; the only hard data are per-broker CFD loss disclosures (eToro UK 51%), a Management Science experiment showing copy trading "leads to excessive risk taking" (35% copied; 88% of those copied the riskiest trader), and MQL5's own rules and forum admissions that fees are kept even if a signal "blows the account in day one or two" and that providers come under "pressure" after gathering subscribers.

### Cited Findings
- Apesteguia, Oechssler & Weidenholzer (2020), "Copy Trading", Management Science 66(12):5608-5622: "information on the success of others leads to a significant increase in risk taking of subjects. This increase in risk taking is even larger when subjects are provided with the option to directly copy others. We conclude that copy trading leads to excessive risk taking." "35% of participants in COPY chose to copy someone in the list, and, of these, 88% copied somebody who had chosen the riskiest possible asset in Block 1." "The more risk-averse subjects are, the more likely they are to copy others. Ironically, it is thus those with a revealed low tolerance for risk taking who are enticed through copy trading to take on more risk." (Laboratory experiment, not live-account data.) — [Paper PDF](https://jose-apesteguia.github.io/CopyTrading.pdf); [DOI](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2019.3508)
- MQL5 Signals mechanics: trades are copied as a percentage of the subscriber's deposit (a fixed volume ratio was rejected because "such a system could not guarantee the security of the Subscriber's deposit"); a 1-lot provider trade may become 1.6 lots for one subscriber and 0.07 for another; synchronisation only automatic when the provider's floating P/L is ≤ 0; "the Signals Provider may just wait out temporary drawdown having a large volume order, while the Subscriber may lose all the funds with all his or her positions closed by Stop Out"; MetaQuotes "does not interfere with relations between Subscribers and Signals Providers"; when an account is added as a provider "its ENTIRE history will ALWAYS be displayed." — [MQL5 Articles 618](https://www.mql5.com/en/articles/618)
- MQL5 Signals rules: "subscriber accepts all execution risks"; "no past trading successes of a selected Signals Source can guarantee future profits"; provider receives payment "less 20% commission retained by the 'Signals' service". — [search summary, MQL5 Signals rules](https://www.mql5.com/en/signals/rules)
- MQL5 forum (2022): "MQL5 still takes you the subscription fee, even if the signal blows the account in day one or two" (Mugur); providers may be reckless because "they will cover their lost money with the subscribers' money"; moderator: the max-drawdown protection setting exists but is "rarely being used by subscribers"; advice: "Stick around for 6 months, follow some of your favorite signals on paper without subscription." — [MQL5 forum 386750](https://www.mql5.com/en/forum/386750)
- Live example of subscriber outcomes: MagicGW audcad L (7 subscribers at time of reading) — platform suspended subscriptions; reviews report "roughly 50% floating losses", closed losses of $480 and $596, "lose 1/3 of my account" (see KQ1). — [MQL5 Signals 2280569](https://www.mql5.com/en/signals/2280569)
- Copy-trading concentration (secondary summary of a peer-reviewed study): the top 5% of copied traders accounted for 61.1% of copier relationships on ZuluTrade and 92.8% on eToro. — [search summary](https://carlsonschool.umn.edu/sites/carlsonschool.umn.edu/files/inline-files/Social_Trading_0.pdf)
- eToro UK's CFD disclosure: 51% of retail accounts lose (lowest of 14 UK brokers, Apr 2026) — a platform-wide figure, not a copier-specific one. — [The Investors Centre](https://www.theinvestorscentre.co.uk/trading/statistics/cfd-trading/)
- Execution drag in copy trading: "A 150ms delay" between master and broker routing; copying "on a standard account with a 1.2-pip spread markup"; "entry orders slip unfavorably while take-profits rarely slip favorably." — [FXNX Verify](https://fxnx.com/en/blog/forex-signal-providers-verify-track-record-before-you-copy)
- Myfxbook AutoTrade: a review states "subscribers can lose their money in the event of unsuccessful trades by the strategy provider" and that Myfxbook is not affiliated with any broker (page could not be read in full; search summary). — [Traders Union](https://tradersunion.com/interesting-articles/myfxbook-autotrade/)

### Inferences
- Scalping signals are the worst case for copy trading because execution drag (latency, spread mark-up, asymmetric slippage) is largest relative to a 5-10-pip target, and because martingale scalpers' large late-basket orders are precisely the trades most likely to be rejected or stopped out on a smaller subscriber account.
- The Apesteguia result implies platform leaderboards themselves (sorted by return) systematically steer copiers toward the highest-risk, highest-"win-rate" providers — the martingale profile.

### Gaps
- No 2025-2026 dataset of MQL5/ZuluTrade/eToro/AutoTrade subscriber P&L was found; eToro's and ZuluTrade's own disclosures were not retrieved. Myfxbook AutoTrade's current status and provider criteria could not be confirmed (403).
- The Carlson School social-trading paper was not read directly.

## KQ7. What the few long-lived, verified scalping systems have in common

### Takeaway
The systems that survive multiple years share: a hard stop-loss on every trade, no martingale/grid, small fixed or percentage sizing (0.01-0.02 lots on small accounts, ≤2-5% risk), modest and *uneven* returns (e.g., +127% in 2022 then +6% in 2023), equity drawdowns typically 15-40% that are openly shown, 3+ years of badge-verified Myfxbook history, and explicit broker/latency sensitivity — i.e., the opposite of a smooth 100%-win curve. Caveat: every source found is vendor-affiliated; no independent audit of a scalping EA was located.

### Cited Findings
- Evening Scalper Pro (M5 mean-reversion on eight crosses, 19:00-23:00 broker time, $2,200): stop losses "Yes, on every trade"; "doesn't use the Martingale method or what's called a grid system"; "3+ years on MyFXBook"; best account started with $150 and grew "141% since December 2021"; +127% in 2022, "As of 2023, it's at a modest 6%"; 2023 months typically +2% to +3% with some near -2%; max drawdown reported inconsistently (~15.62% Jul 2022 vs "~41%" in the comparison table); average loss -16.1 pips vs average win 10.6 pips (i.e., win rate must exceed ~60% to profit); current lot 0.01; risk settings fixed lot / 2% / 5% per trade cap; requires sub-10ms latency and advises testing 20-30 manual cross-pair trades — "If slippage is consistently above 1 pip, the broker may be unsuitable"; results "may not replicate on budget brokers"; 14-day trial, 30-day money-back. (Reviewer runs an affiliate programme; article dated 23 Nov 2023 but updated.) — [Algo Trading Space review](https://algotradingspace.com/evening-scalper-pro-review)
- Realistic return bands cited by reviewers: legitimate EAs claim "2% to 5% (conservative) or 8% to 15% (aggressive)" monthly; Forex Flex Robot "+3.85% monthly on Myfxbook"; a verified account "running since June 2020 grew from $45,103 to $72,872, a 61% gain" (note: the site's "3.29% monthly" figure does not reconcile with 61% over ~5 years); legitimate EAs disclose e.g. "Maximum historical drawdown: 18%" and "60% winning trades over 24 months". — [NYC Servers](https://newyorkcityservers.com/blog/how-to-spot-forex-ea-scams)
- Quantitative acceptance bands: profit factor 1.50-2.20 (above 3.0 on large samples is suspicious); Sortino 2.0+ excellent; recovery factor ≥3.0 over 12 months; 500+ closed trades over 12-24 months; performance across "Trend + Range + Shock" regimes; expectancy positive after commissions and spreads. — [FXNX Verify](https://fxnx.com/en/blog/forex-signal-providers-verify-track-record-before-you-copy)
- Monthly claims above 15% "fail the smell test"; at least five risk-related settings (fixed lots, drawdown limits, news filters). — [ForexCracked](https://www.forexcracked.com/forex-ea/how-to-spot-forex-ea-scams-checklist/)
- Longevity with openly shown drawdown (grid, not scalping, but illustrative of honest disclosure): Waka Waka "4ndr345" real EUR account since May 2023, +80.27% gain, 35.24% max drawdown, no withdrawals — labelled "conservative" by the analyst, who still concludes the EA "is not suitable for most retail traders". — [Oreshnikbot](https://oreshnikbot.com/waka-waka-ea-myfxbook-analysis/)
- Night/Asian-session scalpers with multi-year records are repeatedly cited (Night Hunter Pro "+215% since October 2020, approximately 30% maximum drawdown"; Evening Scalper Pro) but sources conflict on track-record length and drawdown; "many fail once real spreads, slippage and changing market conditions hit them." — [search summaries, EA review sites](https://newyorkcityservers.com/blog/best-forex-scalping-eas)
- Spread/latency sensitivity is structural: strategies relying on trades under 5 pips "are likely to fail live due to latency"; retail latency 50-300 ms; spreads can spike 500%+ at Asian rollover — exactly when night scalpers trade. — [MT4 Programming](https://mt4programming.com/beyond-the-90-myth-why-mt4-strategy-tester-results-fail-in-live-markets-and-how-to-fix-it/)
- Even honest martingale guidance caps exposure: risk no more than "about 5% of your account" per martingale sequence; "some brokers and most prop-firm challenges don't allow martingale or grid systems." — [Forexop](https://forexop.com/martingale-trading-system-overview/)

### Inferences
- A legitimate scalper's record is *lumpy* (a strong year followed by a near-flat year), has a visible string of small losses (win rate well below 100%, average loss larger than average win), and degrades on worse brokers — all features a vendor optimising for sales would hide. Their absence in a sales page is itself a signal.
- Because legitimate scalpers' edges are a few pips, the realistic ceiling on a non-martingale retail scalper appears to be low-single-digit monthly returns with 15-40% equity drawdowns, far from "passive income" narratives.

### Gaps
- No independently audited (non-vendor, non-affiliate) multi-year live record of a retail scalping EA was found; every "verified" claim traces to Myfxbook pages controlled by the vendor or to review sites with affiliate programmes.
- No study quantifies the survival rate of scalping EAs sold on MQL5 Market over, say, three years.
