## KQ1. Which MT4/MT5 brokers are best for scalping (raw/ECN accounts), what spreads/commissions do they publish, and what independent execution data exists?

### Takeaway
The de-facto standard for a scalping-grade MT5 raw account in 2025-26 is 0.0-0.1 pip EURUSD plus USD 3.00-3.50 per lot per side (USD 6-7 round turn); IC Markets, Pepperstone, Tickmill, FP Markets and Exness are the names that recur. Independent measurement shows real demo-account execution of ~75-150 ms with the broker's own "~30 ms"/"<40 ms" claims measuring only the internal processing leg; the only independent multi-broker spread sample found (FastBull, April 2025) puts IC Markets' average XAUUSD spread at 11.6 points with a worst-case of 106 points.

### Inferences
- The gap between broker claims (Pepperstone ~30 ms, IC "<40 ms", FXCM 17 ms) and CompareForexBrokers' measured 77-189 ms for the same brokers is explained by definition: brokers quote internal processing only ("when they hit our servers", "excludes internet latency"), while the independent test measures client-observed round trip minus ping on demo. Neither captures live-liquidity slippage. A scalper should budget ~100-150 ms client-observed round trip on a normal connection, not 30 ms.
- Using FastBull's April 2025 averages, the all-in cost of a 1-lot EURUSD round trip at IC Markets Raw is roughly 0.21 pip spread + $7 commission = ~$9.10 (~0.9 pip); at a 2-5 pip target, costs consume 20-45% of gross edge. For XAUUSD, 11.6 points ($0.116 at $0.01/point on a 2-decimal quote) + $7 commission ≈ $18.60 per lot round trip before slippage.
- Worst-case spread columns (IC Markets EURUSD worst 250 points = 25 pips; XAUUSD worst 106 points) show that even top ECN feeds blow out by 50-100x intermittently, which is fatal for tight-stop scalps if they coincide with a position.
- No source found scores brokers on slippage independently in 2025-26; all slippage data is self-reported. The report writer should present execution quality as "unverified below the broker-claim level".

## KQ2. Which brokers prohibit or restrict scalping, latency arbitrage or very short holds, what are the terms, and were profits voided in 2025-26?

### Takeaway
No major MT5 retail ECN broker was found that bans scalping — IC Markets, Tickmill and Pepperstone explicitly advertise it — so in 2025-26 the hold-time and "toxic flow" restrictions live almost entirely in prop-firm terms, where they are enforced by profit deduction or forfeiture at payout review; the clearest 2025 voiding case is FundingTicks' December 2025 retroactive one-minute minimum hold.

### Cited Findings
- IC Markets: platforms "have no restrictions on trading", explicitly cites scalping and high-frequency trading; "no minimum order distance and a freeze level of 0". — [IC Markets](https://ic.com/trading-accounts/standard-account)
- Tickmill: "Execute scalping, hedging, and automated trading strategies across all account types". — [Tickmill](https://www.tickmill.com/accounts)
- FTMO Forbidden Trading Practices (official): bans strategies that "exploit errors in our Services" including price-display errors and "delays in their updates, or an external or slow data feed"; bans "software, artificial intelligence, ultra-high-speed tools, or mass data entry" giving an unfair advantage; bans opposite positions "between connected accounts" for manipulative purposes; bans gap trading around major news/market closes; EAs causing "more than 2,000 server requests per day" or "overload of the trading server". Consequences: "removal of simulated trades from your history, restricted access to a trading platform ... forfeiture of any potential Rewards, or even termination of all agreements". No minimum holding time stated. — [FTMO](https://ftmo.com/en/forbidden-trading-practices/)
- FTMO strategies FAQ: "Your trading style is up to you"; styles must be "replicable on live accounts"; server limits "200 orders at a time" and "2000 max positions per day". — [FTMO FAQ](https://ftmo.com/en/faq/which-instruments-can-i-trade-and-what-strategies-am-i-allowed-to-use/)
- FundingPips (secondary rules compilation updated 2026-09-16): prohibited strategies listed as "High-frequency trading, server spamming, latency arbitrage, toxic flow, tick scalping, gap trading"; "Forbidden strategies lead to immediate termination"; no official minimum hold time, but author advises >60 s because very short holds "can be flagged"; hedging listed as forbidden (cross-account), single-account hedging unaddressed. — [proptradingvibes](https://www.proptradingvibes.com/blog/fundingpips-rules)
- FundedNext (FXEmpire review, updated 2025-09-11): "High-frequency trading (HFT) is not permitted"; latency-arbitrage bots and gap trading banned; risk limits of 5 open positions and 50 trades per day described as anti-HFT measures. — [FXEmpire](https://fxempire.com/prop-firms/fundednext)
- Per-firm hold-time rules compiled 2026-02-24 (affiliate site): FTMO no minimum; FundedNext "tick scalping (trades lasting seconds) prohibited", no number; The5ers trades under 10 seconds prohibited; BrightFunded at least 50% of trades >1 minute; InstantFunding trades must last >60 s, HFT prohibited (2 violations = warning + deduction, 3rd = breach); E8 no more than 50% of trades under 1 minute; TopOne Trader profitable trades held ≥5 minutes; Goat Funded Trader profits from trades under 2 minutes removed; Lark no minimum. — [thegodfunded](https://thegodfunded.com/en/blog/best-prop-firms-for-scalpers)
- FundingTicks (futures arm of FundingPips' owner), reported 2025-12-23: added a one-minute minimum trade hold (previously none), raised daily profit requirement to $200 from $150, profitable days to six from five, cut profit split to 80% from up to 90%, capped withdrawals; traders say it was applied retroactively to existing accounts and "previously valid trades were invalidated"; one trader's account went from ~$3.2k profit to $751.62; Trustpilot fell 4.1 -> 3.2 with 38% one-star; CEO Khaled (also FundingPips) said he had paid out >$220M; firm did not respond. — [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/prop-firm-fundingticks-faces-massive-backlash-after-retroactive-rule-change/); also covered by [FastBull](https://www.fastbull.com/brokersview/news/retroactive-rule-shock-fundingticks-sparks-trader-outrage-after-sudden-terms-change-318381)
- Smaller firms' rule pages (search snippets; pages returned 404/403 when fetched): TradeXMastery "all trades must be held for a minimum of 3 minutes", profits from violating trades "may be forfeited" — [TradeXMastery](https://intercom.help/tradexmastery-help-center/en/articles/14174215-scalping); Winbance: if scalping detected during payout review "the reward will be canceled and the account deactivated" — [Winbance](https://winbance.featurebase.app/help/articles/13217001-is-scalping-allowed); EverBlue Trader "6 minute trade duration rule" — [EverBlue](https://intercom.help/everbluetrader/en/articles/12091816-6-minute-trade-duration-rule); TraderScale: third-party dealing team reviews all activity at payout, violations lead to "account closure and forfeiture of profits" — [TraderScale](https://intercom.help/traderscale/en/articles/9561450-speedy-prohibited-trading-practices); Tycoon Funded: "no minimum hold time" but duration "can be a key indicator" of compliance — [Tycoon](https://intercom.help/tycoon-funded/en/articles/10372885-what-is-the-minimum-hold-time-for-a-trade)
- "Toxic flow" definition in the retail/market-making context: hyperscalpers' market orders create "toxic order flow — trades statistically likely to lose money for the market maker"; in spot FX, LPs profile counterparties and may refuse flow deemed toxic. — [Mometic blog (Schwab hyperscalper closures)](https://blog.mometic.com/why-schwab-is-closing-accounts-for-hyperscalpers-insights-from-traders-and-market-realities); [BabyPips thread](https://forums.babypips.com/t/toxic-flow-and-illegal-trading-methods/204994) (snippets)
- Prop-firm closures 2020-23 attributed ~15% to "rule changes that deny payouts" (site's own estimate, unaudited). — [ThePropFirmGuide, updated 2026-07-13](https://thepropfirmguide.com/prop-firm-statistics/)

### Inferences
- For a live retail account at a raw-spread ECN, scalping restrictions are effectively nil on paper; the practical constraint is cost and execution, not permission. The permission risk concentrates in prop firms, where "tick scalping", "HFT" and "toxic flow" are undefined terms enforced at payout review — so any scalping technique recommended for prop accounts must keep average hold times above the strictest common threshold (1-3 minutes) and trade counts under 50/day to be safe across firms.
- FTMO's 2,000 server requests/day and 200 simultaneous orders limits are the only quantified "HFT" thresholds found at a tier-1 firm; an EA sending modify/close requests per tick will breach this quickly.

### Gaps
- No 2025-26 case was found of a regulated retail (non-prop) MT5 broker voiding scalping profits; a FastBull complaint about "Vortex FX" ($16,000) surfaced in search but could not be verified and is not a recognised broker. Search budget ran out before targeted searches on broker profit-cancellation disputes could be run.
- Exact definitions of "tick scalping"/"toxic flow" in FundingPips' and FundedNext's own T&Cs were not retrieved (official help centres returned index pages only).

## KQ3. Prop-firm rules (FTMO, The5ers, FundedNext, FundingPips, E8) on scalping, news, holding time, consistency, EAs; MT4/MT5 availability after MetaQuotes' 2024 crackdown; pass/payout stats

### Takeaway
Tier-1 prop firms allow scalping and EAs but gate funded-account profits with news windows (FTMO 2 min, FundingPips 5 min, FundedNext 5 min at 40% credit), consistency caps (FTMO 50%/30% best-day, FundingPips 35%/15%), and anti-HFT request limits; MT5 returned to most non-US clients in early 2025 via direct MetaQuotes licences, while in the US only FTMO offers MT5 (via OANDA, since Aug 2025) and cTrader also closed to US props in 2026. Published pass rates cluster at 5-10% with ~1-7% of entrants ever receiving a payout; tracked 2025 payouts were ~$325M led by FundedNext ($107.8M) and FundingPips ($97.1M).

### Cited Findings

**FTMO (official pages)**
- 1-Step: profit target 10%; Max Daily Loss 3% (reset 00:00 CE(S)T); Max Loss 10% end-of-day trailing; consistency: Best Day must not exceed 50% of total profit (accounts ordered on/after 2026-10-12; legacy rule measured against Positive Days' Profit). 2-Step: 10% then 5%; Max Daily Loss 5%; Max Loss 10% static; minimum 4 trading days. FTMO Instant: Max Daily Loss 3%, Max Loss 10% intraday balance-based, Best Day ≤30% of total profit. — [FTMO Trading Objectives](https://ftmo.com/en/trading-objectives/)
- News: "Restrictions for trading during selected news releases apply only to the Standard account type"; "restricts order execution for 2 minutes before and 2 minutes after selected news releases"; applies "only to symbols that include the affected currency/symbol" (USD pairs, XAUUSD, US indices, DXY, crude oil listed); Challenge/Verification and Swing accounts exempt; "Violating this rule may result in termination of an FTMO Account / FTMO Instant Account". — [FTMO FAQ](https://ftmo.com/en/faq/can-i-trade-news/)
- Strategies: "no reason for limiting or restricting your trading strategy"; EAs allowed; third-party EAs risk hitting the "maximum capital allocation rule"; server limits 200 orders at a time / 2000 positions per day. — [FTMO FAQ](https://ftmo.com/en/faq/which-instruments-can-i-trade-and-what-strategies-am-i-allowed-to-use/)
- Platforms: MT5, MT4, cTrader, TradingView (TradingView "via the OANDA Broker Profile"); news/weekend restrictions "apply only to Standard accounts". — [FTMO platforms](https://ftmo.com/en/trading-platforms/)
- Third-party summary (2026-02-21) agrees: no news restriction in Challenge/Verification; 2-minute rule on funded Standard; swing exempt; EAs allowed. — [TradingFinder](https://tradingfinder.com/props/ftmo/rules)
- FTMO CEO stated (Sep 2025) >$450M paid since launch; FTMO not in Prop Firm Match tracker. — [Finance Magnates, 2026-01-06](https://financemagnates.com/forex/prop-firm-match-tracked-about-325-million-in-payouts-to-traders-in-2025)

## KQ5. Measurable impact of VPS/latency on scalping outcomes; MQL5 VPS vs third-party; when it matters

### Takeaway
The VPS hop is the smallest term in the latency budget: MQL5 VPS advertises 0-5 ms (96% of brokers <10 ms) and IC Markets claims <1 ms to colocated VPS, while broker-side processing is 30-150 ms and spikes to 1,000-2,800 ms have been logged at a tier-1 ECN in Sep 2026; a VPS mainly removes the 100-250 ms home-connection penalty and disconnect risk, and only matters materially for strategies whose edge is measured in sub-100 ms (news spikes, latency arbitrage) which prop firms ban anyway.

### Cited Findings
- MQL5 VPS official claims: "more than thirty hosting points"; 96% of broker servers reachable in under 10 ms, 84% under 3 ms; "0-5 ms latency" vs home-PC "100 ms or more"; price $15/month (1 mo), $13 (3 mo), $10.83 (6 mo), $10 (12 mo); 24-hour free trial; up to 3 GB RAM, 16 GB disk; 99.99% uptime claim; measurement method not stated. — [MQL5 VPS](https://www.mql5.com/en/vps)
- MQL5 forum (2021-04): user measured ~5 ms vs advertised 2.39 ms; moderator: "execution with most brokers takes about 30-50 ms, so 2 ms matters little"; another user: typical latency 30-50 ms on FTTC lines, 2-10 ms on FTTH. — [MQL5 forum 367498](https://mql5.com/en/forum/367498)
- MQL5 forum (2026-09-03): IC Markets client on third-party VPS in same region, ping to access server <1 ms, observed 2,800 ms between "market buy" and "accepted"; over several days gaps ranged from tens of ms to a few hundred, ~1,000 ms and the 2,800 ms spike; moderator: ping measures only reach to the access server, not order processing; another user: "almost certainly broker-side". — [MQL5 forum 515532](https://www.mql5.com/en/forum/515532)

- IC Markets: "latency of less than 1 millisecond to major VPS providers"; MT4/MT5 servers in Equinix NY4 with cross-connects to price providers. — [IC Markets](https://ic.com/global/en/trading-accounts/raw-spread-account); [IC servers](https://ic.com/au/en/forex-trading-tools/trading-servers)

### Inferences
- Latency budget for an MT5 market order: network to access server (home 100-250 ms; VPS 0-5 ms) + broker processing (30-150 ms typical, spikes to seconds) + LP fill. A VPS cuts the first term by ~100-200 ms; it cannot touch the second. For scalps targeting ≥5-10 pips / ≥$1 on gold with ≥1-minute holds, the difference between 5 ms and 100 ms network latency is rarely the deciding variable; it becomes decisive only when competing for a specific price in the first seconds after a release — the exact behaviour prop firms prohibit.
- The practical case for a VPS is uptime and consistency (no home-connection drops while a scalp with a tight stop is open), not raw speed; MQL5 VPS at $10-15/month is cheap relative to a single slipped stop on 1 lot of gold.
- Measure, don't assume: MT5 journal timestamps between "market buy" and "accepted" expose broker-side processing; traders should log this across sessions and news before trusting any broker's ms claim.

### Gaps

- MQL5 VPS latency measurement methodology is undisclosed; whether it is one-way or round-trip is disputed in forums.
- No third-party VPS provider (Beeks, ForexVPS, NYC Servers) published latency data was read in full.

## KQ6. MT5-specific features that matter for scalpers (real-tick tester, hedging vs netting, DOM, fill policies, execution modes, stops level) and the MT4 phase-out status in 2026

### Takeaway
MT5's "Every tick based on real ticks" mode is the only MetaTrader backtest that replays broker tick data with intra-minute spread changes — generated-tick modes apply a fixed per-minute spread and generate no intrabar ticks when tick volume is 1-2 — so scalping backtests on anything else are unreliable; MT5 also exposes execution mode, fill policy (FOK/IOC/BOC/Return), stops/freeze level, DOM depth and hedging/netting margin mode per symbol/account. MT4 has had no new licences since January 2018 and no development since 2016, and brokers such as OANDA Japan are now shutting it off (final MT4 cut-off 2026-11-27, citing no MetaQuotes maintenance), but no global MetaQuotes end-of-life date exists.

### Cited Findings
- Tick generation modes (official MT5 help): "Every tick" generates OHLC plus intermediate ticks from reference points — "most accurate" of generated modes but slowest; "1 Minute OHLC" generates only 4 prices per minute; "Open Prices Only" runs OnTick only at bar open, "stop levels and pending orders may trigger at prices different from those specified"; "Mathematical Calculations" runs no ticks. Real ticks: broker-accumulated ticks "from exchanges and liquidity providers" stored monthly as TKC files; "as close to real conditions as possible"; "The spread may change within a minute bar". Generated modes: "Ask equals Bid plus that fixed spread" of the minute bar, so intra-minute spread changes are not modelled; bars with tick volume 1-2 get no intermediate ticks; if a minute has no tick data the tester falls back to generated ticks. — [MetaTrader 5 help](https://www.metatrader5.com/en/terminal/help/algotrading/tick_generation)
- Forum: a scalping EA profitable in "Every tick" kept hitting stop-loss under real-tick testing (anecdote). — [MQL5 forum 355663](https://mql5.com/en/forum/355663) (snippet); real-ticks mode "runs about 10 times slower". — [MQL5 blog 769442](https://www.mql5.com/en/blogs/post/769442) (snippet)
- Symbol properties (MQL5 docs): SYMBOL_TRADE_STOPS_LEVEL "Minimal indention in points from the current close price to place Stop orders"; SYMBOL_TRADE_FREEZE_LEVEL "Distance to freeze trade operations in points"; SYMBOL_FILLING_MODE flags FOK ("executed in the specified volume only"), IOC (fill available, cancel rest), BOC (book-or-cancel, limit/stop-limit only), Return (remaining volume stays working); SYMBOL_TRADE_EXEMODE: Request, Instant ("If the broker rejects the price, it returns a requote"), Market ("A broker makes a decision about the order execution price without any additional discussion with the trader"), Exchange; SYMBOL_SPREAD_FLOAT; SYMBOL_TICKS_BOOKDEPTH ("For symbols that have no queue of requests, the value is equal to zero"); SYMBOL_SWAP_MODE variants (points, deposit currency, interest, reopen) and SYMBOL_SWAP_ROLLOVER3DAYS. — [MQL5 docs](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/constants/environment_state/marketinfoconstants)
- Account margin modes (MQL5 docs): ACCOUNT_MARGIN_MODE_RETAIL_NETTING — one position per symbol; ACCOUNT_MARGIN_MODE_RETAIL_HEDGING — "multiple positions can exist for one symbol", margin uses SYMBOL_MARGIN_HEDGED; ACCOUNT_FIFO_CLOSE forces oldest-first closing and is always false outside hedging mode; ACCOUNT_HEDGE_ALLOWED flags opposite positions. — [MQL5 docs](https://www.mql5.com/en/docs/constants/environment_state/accountinformation)
- IC Markets: "no minimum order distance and a freeze level of 0" (MT4/MT5); hedging allowed, no FIFO. — [IC Markets](https://ic.com/trading-accounts/standard-account)
- FTMO: MT5 offers "Depth of Market and six pending order types, including stop-limit orders". — [FTMO platforms](https://ftmo.com/en/trading-platforms/)
- MT4 licensing: "MetaQuotes stopped selling MT4 in January 2018"; June 2021 more companies on MT5 than MT4; Q4 2020 MT4 still 84.5% of MetaQuotes-platform volume; end-2020 overall market MT4 ~66%, MT5 12.7%, other 21.3%; basic MT5 server from $5,000; MT5 ~7M lines of code vs 2M for MT4. — [Finance Magnates, 2021-06-11](https://financemagnates.com/forex/analysis/mt5-will-phase-out-mt4-but-it-will-take-time)
- MetaQuotes (Nov 2016): "We do not expect to see any developments to come out of MT4 ever again"; "MT4 is slowly dying out". — [Finance Magnates, 2016-11-15](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/technology/metaquotes-emphasizing-mt5-development-as-mt4-slowly-dying-out/)
- OANDA Japan MT4 shutdown (reported 2026-03-27): new MT4 sub-accounts suspended; new order placement halts after 2026-09-25; MT4 ends at close 2026-11-27; reasons: "cybersecurity requirements", "lack of ongoing maintenance", MT4 "no longer covered by MetaQuotes maintenance"; 2024 OANDA Japan had already closed two MT4 servers. — [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/oanda-japan-pushes-clients-to-mt5-as-it-sets-mt4-shutdown/)
- MetaQuotes raised MT4/MT5 fees 25% at start of 2025. — [Finance Magnates, 2025-03-17](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/funding-pips-becomes-latest-prop-firm-to-bring-metatrader-5-back-after-year-long-hiatus)
- MT4 still offered in 2026 by FTMO (MT4 on iOS/Android/Windows/macOS/web) and Tickmill (Classic/Raw on MT4 and MT5). — [FTMO platforms](https://ftmo.com/en/trading-platforms/); [Tickmill](https://www.tickmill.com/accounts)
- FundedNext allows EAs "on MT4 and MT5 only" (not on Match-Trader/cTrader). — [FXEmpire](https://fxempire.com/prop-firms/fundednext)

### Inferences
- For scalping validation, MT5 real-tick backtesting is the minimum bar; MT4's tester (synthetic ticks, fixed spread unless third-party tick data is imported) cannot model intra-minute spread blowouts that decide gold scalps. This alone argues for MT5 over MT4 for any scalper in 2026.
- Hedging mode on MT5 (the default at most retail CFD brokers) lets a scalper layer independent entries with independent stops; netting (exchange-style) aggregates them. Prop firms that forbid "hedging" generally mean opposite positions, which MT5 hedging mode makes possible — the EA must avoid it explicitly.
- "Market" execution mode (standard on ECN raw accounts) means no requotes but uncontrolled slippage; "Instant" mode gives price certainty via requotes but can leave a scalper unfilled. Fill policy must be read from SYMBOL_FILLING_MODE at runtime — sending FOK to a broker that supports only IOC/Return is a classic EA failure.
- MT4 is in managed decline rather than hard shutdown: brokers can keep running existing licences, but a 2026 scalper building new tooling on MT4 faces shrinking broker support (OANDA Japan precedent), higher broker costs (+25% fees) and no security patches.

### Gaps
- No official MetaQuotes announcement of a global MT4 end-of-life date was found. A claim that MetaQuotes blocked desktop terminals below a minimum build in July 2025 appeared only on SEO blogs and is unverified.
- Current (2025-26) MT4 vs MT5 market-share figures were not found from a reliable source; the 2020 split is the latest hard data read.
- Per-broker stops-level values for XAUUSD (other than IC Markets' zero) were not collected.

## KQ7. Session/time-of-day conditions for scalping (London open, NY open, overlap, Asian, pre-news)

### Takeaway
Sources agree the London-New York overlap (~13:00-16:30 UTC, 08:00-11:30 ET) carries the deepest liquidity and largest clean moves, London open (~07:00-08:00 UTC) is second, and the dangerous windows are ±2-5 minutes around 08:30 ET / 14:00 ET US releases, the first 5-15 minutes after the NY open when gold spreads can double or triple, the post-16:00 ET fade and the 17:00 ET rollover when spreads widen 3-10x; however, every per-session spread figure found is vendor or SEO content, not independent data.

News: NFP first Friday 08:30 ET; CPI mid-month 08:30 ET; FOMC statement 14:00 ET + presser 14:30 ET; PPI/retail sales/claims/GDP at 08:30 ET. ## KQ8. Regulatory leverage limits (ESMA, FCA, ASIC, US, offshore) and how they constrain scalping position sizing and returns

### Takeaway
Retail leverage is capped at 30:1 on major FX and 20:1 on gold/indices in the EU (ESMA 2018), UK (FCA since Aug 2019) and Australia (ASIC order running to 23 May 2027 with a 2026-27 consultation to extend), 50:1/20:1 in the US (CFTC/NFA; secondary source), 25:1 in Japan, while offshore entities of the same brokers (Seychelles, Mauritius, SVG, BVI) advertise 500:1 to 1:5000 or "unlimited" — so an identically-sized gold scalp needs 25-250x more margin on a regulated account, and the 50% margin close-out rule makes high-frequency, tight-stop scalping on small regulated accounts capital-constrained rather than edge-constrained.

### Cited Findings
- ESMA (agreed 2018-03-23, announced 2018-03-27): leverage limits at position opening — 30:1 major FX; 20:1 non-major FX, gold and major indices; 10:1 commodities other than gold and non-major indices; 5:1 individual equities; 2:1 crypto; margin close-out at 50% of minimum required margin per account; negative balance protection per account; standardised risk warning with the provider's loss percentage; NCA analyses found 74-89% of retail CFD accounts lose money, average losses €1,600-€29,000 per client. — [ESMA press release](https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-agrees-prohibit-binary-options-and-restrict-cfds-protect-retail-investors)
- FCA PS19/18: leverage "between 30:1 and 2:1 depending on the volatility of the underlying asset"; effective 1 August 2019 (CFDs) and 1 September 2019 (CFD-like options); close-out when funds "fall to 50% of the margin needed"; negative balance protection; standardised loss warning. — [FCA](https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/policy-statements/ps19-18-restricting-contract-difference-products-sold-retail-clients)
- ASIC product intervention order: in force since 29 March 2021; extended five years to 23 May 2027; leverage ratios "from 30:1 to 2:1"; 91% reduction in aggregate net retail losses from $372M to $33M per quarter on average in first six months; standardised margin close-out, negative balance protection, inducement ban. — [ASIC 22-082MR](https://asic.gov.au/about-asic/news-centre/find-a-media-release/2022-releases/22-082mr-asic-s-cfd-product-intervention-order-extended-for-five-years/). ASIC's original tiers: 30:1 major FX, 20:1 minor FX/gold/major indices, 5:1 shares, 2:1 crypto. — [LiquidityFinder](https://liquidityfinder.com/news/asic-confirms-cfd-leverage-and-marketing-restrictions-come-into-effect-today-0ir2g) (snippet)
- ASIC timetable (reported 2026-08-26): review Q3 2026, consultation Q4 2026, ministerial approval Q1 2027, extend or expire Q2 2027; separately ASIC proposes freezing CFD issuer capital requirements to 2032. — [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/regulation/asic-sets-q4-consultation-on-cfd-leverage-rules-ahead-of-2027-expiry/). A secondary report says ASIC floated a 10-year extension to 2037 (proposal, not decided). — [BrokerNews](https://www.brokernews.com.au/news/breaking-news/asic-moves-to-bring-cryptosettled-cfds-under-2037-rules-290132.aspx) (snippet)
- Cross-regulator table (LiquidityFinder, updated 2026-03-07; secondary, unverified against regulators): EU/UK/AU 30:1 major FX, 20:1 minor FX/gold/major indices, 10:1 commodities, 5:1 other indices, 2:1 crypto (UK: retail crypto CFDs banned); Japan 25:1 major FX, 20:1 others; Singapore MAS 50:1 major FX (text says MAS sets no hard caps); South Africa FSCA 200:1 (text says no strict limits); US CFTC/NFA 50:1 major FX, 20:1 minor; Mauritius FSC 100:1 major / 50:1 others; Seychelles FSA no limit. — [LiquidityFinder](https://liquidityfinder.com/insight/industry/comparison-of-cfd-retail-broker-leverage-limits-by-regulator)
- Offshore leverage actually advertised: IC Markets "up to 1:5000" (MT4/MT5, "dynamic and may change"); Tickmill 1:1000; Exness 1:2000/"unlimited"; FxPro 1:10000 (FSA Seychelles); FP Markets 1:500 (FSA/CMA); IronFX 1:2000 (BVI). — [IC Markets](https://ic.com/trading-accounts/standard-account); [Tickmill](https://www.tickmill.com/accounts); [FXEmpire](https://www.fxempire.com/brokers/best/scalping)
- US: CFTC consumer forex advisory contains no leverage figure, only "Margin trading can make you responsible for losses that greatly exceed the dollar amount you deposited". — [CFTC](https://www.cftc.gov/LearnAndProtect/AdvisoriesAndArticles/fraudadv_forex.html). FTMO's US route runs via OANDA Corporation, described as "one of the few US-regulated forex brokers" (RFED). — [Finance Magnates](https://financemagnates.com/forex/prop-trading-on-metatrader-5-is-back-in-the-us)
- Prop firms sidestep leverage caps by being "simulated"/"demo" accounts: FTMO's US site uses "demo trading", "simulated platform", "rewards"; firms adopted "simulated trading"/"virtual funds" language after the CFTC sued My Forex Funds. — [Finance Magnates](https://financemagnates.com/forex/prop-trading-on-metatrader-5-is-back-in-the-us)

### Inferences
- Margin per 1 lot XAUUSD at ~$4,000/oz (illustrative; price not sourced here) is ~$400,000 notional: at 20:1 (EU/UK/AU) ≈ $20,000 margin; at 500:1 ≈ $800; at 1:5000 ≈ $80. A $10,000 regulated retail account can therefore hold at most ~0.5 lot of gold, so a $1.00 scalp yields ~$50 (0.5%) before ~$9-10 costs — regulated-leverage scalping is a low-absolute-return activity unless the account is large, which is why prop "simulated" accounts (effectively 1:100 with no regulator cap) and offshore entities dominate retail scalping in 2025-26.
- The 50% margin close-out rule interacts badly with many simultaneous tight-stop scalps: a brief spread blowout across several open gold positions can trigger a close-out at the worst price even when individual stops have not been hit.
- Jurisdiction choice is a strategy parameter: the same broker (IC Markets, Pepperstone, FP Markets, Tickmill) offers 30:1 under ASIC/CySEC/FCA and 500:1-5000:1 offshore, with different negative-balance protections and recourse.
