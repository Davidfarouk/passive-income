## Key Question 1: On MQL5 Signals, which long-lived (12+ months) signals trade scalping-style, and what do their statistics and histories show?

### Takeaway
Of ~4,275 ranked paid signals visible on MQL5 (1,608 MT4 + 2,667 MT5), only a few dozen are both old (5+ years) and scalping-style (sub-2-hour average hold, 25+ trades/week). The individually examined long-lived scalpers (Scalp IC Markets ECN/MT5, Techno Long Term, HJM1) all show high win rates (52-73%), thin profit factors (1.07-1.80), tiny expected payoffs per trade (EUR 0.17-3.43 on EUR 100-ish accounts), near-zero or negative current monthly growth, and reviews dominated by slippage complaints; the headline multi-thousand-percent "growth" figures come from compounding on EUR 100 initial deposits with repeated withdrawals, not from large dollar profits.

### Inferences
- Approximate compound annual growth from MQL5 growth% and weeks (my arithmetic; MQL5 growth is chain-linked and inflates when early gains compound on EUR 100 deposits, so treat as upper bounds): JINNIUFX7 685% over 10.1 years is ~22.6%/yr with 8% DD; NightVisionEA ICM 344% over 6.8 years is ~24%/yr; Night Hunter Pro All Pairs 140% over 6.1 years is ~15.5%/yr; EEVCC Night IC 6 111% over 5.4 years is ~15%/yr; TP TRADER 2,862% over 6.9 years is ~63%/yr (19% DD, 41 trades/wk); R Factor Mean Reversal 3,441% over 6.8 years is ~69%/yr (41% DD, 98 trades/wk); Scalp IC Markets ECN 8,573% over 7.25 years is ~85%/yr nominally but total dollar profit is EUR 2,908 on EUR 404 deposited, and current monthly growth is negative.
- The 10 oldest MT4 signals with DD <= 25% (JINNIUFX7, Commodity Accumulation Plan, ICTrading Signal 2, SCR EURAUD, Low real risk patient signal, PAI Unique Mode, SCR EURAUD advanced, TP TRADER, NightVisionEA ICM, RFactor Portfolio JM, Night Hunter Pro x2, SFE Portfolio1) have between 0 and 3 subscribers each, while newer high-growth signals with 44-103 weeks of history carry 13-71 subscribers: subscribers follow recent growth, not longevity.
- Four of the five long-lived scalpers I examined have negative current monthly growth (Scalp IC Markets ECN -3.23%, Scalp IC Markets MT5 -4.11%, Techno Long Term -19.61%) or a PF of 1.07 (HJM1), consistent with edge decay or spread/slippage erosion in night-scalping and latency-arb approaches.
- "Night scalper" EAs on exotic crosses (GBPCAD, EURAUD, EURSGD, AUDCAD, CHFJPY) are the dominant surviving scalping archetype on MQL5; gold M1 scalpers are abundant but almost all are under 2 years old.
- The default ranking's floor at -70% growth suggests MQL5 hides or de-lists signals that have lost more than ~70%, so even the visible failure tail understates total failures.

### Gaps
- MQL5 signal pages do not disclose real vs demo; the "Trading history" tab requires login, so grid/martingale behavior could only be inferred from the author's text and summary stats (e.g. max consecutive losses, avg loss/avg win ratio), not from the raw deal list.
- I could not open the pages of JINNIUFX7, TP TRADER, R Factor Mean Reversal, NightVisionEA ICM, Night Hunter Pro, EEVCC Night IC 6 or ICTrading Signal 2 (no signal IDs in the list HTML; MQL5's site search returned no results to a non-JS fetch; WebSearch budget exhausted), so their average holding times, profit factors and methods are unverified.
- Monthly growth tables are not exposed in page text, so year-by-year returns for the long-lived signals could not be recorded.

## Key Question 2: On Myfxbook, which verified real accounts with 1+ year history show scalping with controlled drawdown?

### Takeaway
Myfxbook could not be read: myfxbook.com/systems, individual member system pages and even the help page returned HTTP 403 to automated fetches. Search snippets surfaced several "Scalping" tagged systems, but none could be verified for real/demo status, verification badges, age or drawdown, so no Myfxbook account can be listed as a verified long-lived scalper from this research.

### Cited Findings
- Myfxbook's own help page states that Track Record verification means the account history on Myfxbook matches the broker's history, and Trading Privileges verification shows the page owner actually controls the account (as summarized in search results; the page itself returned 403) — [Myfxbook help](https://www.myfxbook.com/help/?p=300)
- Search surfaced Myfxbook "Scalping" systems: G1-Scalping-RiskWay (tipok123), Scalping Yew (maxtrader2021), Scalping 1min (thepscalping), Scalping Signal EurUsd FREE (rajausd), Stop-Strategy $756.94 - Scalping (tipok123), PRO SCALPER STRATEGY (MarjanRijavec). Snippets showed one scalping system with 29.98% drawdown and roughly $27 net gain on ~$409 deposited, one with track record verified but trading privileges not verified, and one whose stats cover only the last 200 transactions — [Myfxbook search results](https://www.myfxbook.com/members/thepscalping/scalping-1min/1705991)
- Forum guides on reading Myfxbook records warn that owners can mark demo accounts as real and that "Track record not verified" is a red flag — [BabyPips: ways to recognize fake and real accounts on Myfxbook](https://forums.babypips.com/t/ways-to-recognize-fake-and-real-accounts-on-myfxbook/194062)

### Inferences
- None can be drawn about Myfxbook scalper performance from this research; the snippets that did appear (tiny net gains, ~30% DD, partial verification) are consistent with the MQL5 pattern of small accounts and thin edges but are not evidence.

### Gaps
- Myfxbook blocks non-browser access (HTTP 403 on /systems, /members/..., /community/..., /help). The Myfxbook systems leaderboard, "Scalping" strategy filter, and verified-real-account filter were therefore not observed. A browser-based session is needed to fill this.

## Key Question 4: Prop-firm leaderboards and payout announcements 2025-2026: do top earners scalp, and what do published profiles show?

### Takeaway
Prop-firm payout data is large in aggregate ($325M tracked across firms in 2025; FundedNext alone $15.19M to 8,340 traders in Feb 2026) but the only behavioral dataset published (FundedNext, Feb 2026) shows paid CFD traders had a median of 1.5 trades/day with 83.6% trading 1-2 times a day, i.e. most payout recipients are not high-frequency scalpers; firm-published individual profiles do include self-described gold scalpers (FTMO: 572 trades in 10 days for +25.1% on a $200K simulated account with no stop loss on the case-study trade; The5ers: two gold M1 scalpers), but these are curated success stories with no survival follow-up.

Data is "shared directly by firms"; FTMO and The5ers are absent; The5ers has never published payout figures; FTMO's CEO said in Sept 2025 the firm had paid >$450M since launch (published 2026-01-06) — [Finance Magnates](https://financemagnates.com/forex/prop-firm-match-tracked-about-325-million-in-payouts-to-traders-in-2025)

## Key Question 5: What is the survivorship picture for MQL5/Myfxbook signals that were top-ranked 1-2 years ago?

### Takeaway
No one has published a longitudinal count of MQL5 signals that survived from a past top ranking; the best available evidence is the current age distribution (only 96 of ~1,608 MT4 and 48 of ~2,667 MT5 ranked signals are older than ~4.2 years, and a third of those oldest signals show 60%+ drawdowns or negative lifetime growth), a 10-signal audit showing ~80% of profit concentrated in the first 1-7 days, and vendors themselves closing signals after drawdowns.

### Cited Findings
- Age distribution: MT4 signals with >= 246 weeks of history fill exactly two age-sorted pages (96 signals) of a 1,608-signal ranked list; MT5 signals with >= 216 weeks fill one page (48) of a 2,667-signal list — [MT4 by weeks p1](https://www.mql5.com/en/signals/mt4/list/page1?orderby=weeks); [MT4 by weeks p2](https://www.mql5.com/en/signals/mt4/list/page2?orderby=weeks); [MT5 by weeks p1](https://www.mql5.com/en/signals/mt5/list/page1?orderby=weeks); [MT4 p34](https://www.mql5.com/en/signals/mt4/list/page34); [MT5 p56](https://www.mql5.com/en/signals/mt5/list/page56)

## Key Question 6: Among verified long-lived scalpers, what common traits appear?

The surviving multi-year scalping-style records on MQL5 share a profile: automated night-session or mean-reversion scalping on low-volatility crosses (GBPCAD, EURAUD, EURSGD, AUDCAD, CHFJPY) or gold/indices; 25-40 trades per week; 1-2 hour average holds; 63-73% win rates with average losses 1.3-1.9x average wins; profit factors 1.2-1.8; fixed SL/TP claimed by the author; tiny EUR 100-1,000 accounts at 1:200-1:500 leverage; and monthly returns that are now flat to negative, with slippage of 1-2+ pips on copier brokers being the most common complaint.

- Long-lived scalping records on MQL5 are automated (algo 78-100%); the only manual/0%-algo high-frequency record (HJM1) is self-described arbitrage on a cent account and does not copy.
- A realistic "what long-lived scalpers actually make" range from the age-sorted lists is roughly 15-25%/yr with 8-25% max DD (JINNIUFX7, NightVisionEA ICM, Night Hunter Pro, EEVCC Night IC 6), rising to 60-70%/yr nominal only with 19-41% DD and 40-100 trades/week (TP TRADER, R Factor Mean Reversal), with the caveat that these are MQL5 compounding figures on small accounts.

### Gaps
- Average pips per trade is not published by MQL5 (only gross pips per symbol); e.g. Scalp IC ECN gross profit 116,237 pips vs gross loss 57,077 pips over 2,707 trades cannot be converted to a clean net pips/trade without the deal list.
- Session-of-day distribution is not exposed in page text; "night" is from author descriptions only.

## Key Question 7: What 2025-2026 studies or data journalism exist on copy-trading/signal-subscriber returns?

### Takeaway
Every independent 2025-2026 dataset found shows copy-trading followers underperform leaders and that roughly half or fewer are profitable: eToro mirrored trades averaged -61 bps ROI versus +1 bp for non-mirror traders; Followme found 47.27% of copy accounts profitable; a 100,000-outcome crypto study found 48.48% of followers profitable while 97% of leaders had positive personal PnL; and an on-chain study found 6.16% of 292,000 Fomo wallets profitable.

### Cited Findings
- Joseph, Riedl, Pentland & Moro, "When Influence Misleads" (arXiv 2507.01817, July 2025), eToro data: 87.9M trades, 340k traders, 188k mirroring relationships (June 2011-Nov 2013; Methods section gives 87.5M trades, 825k mirrors, Aug 2010-Dec 2013). Average ROI in basis points: all traders -7.67; non-mirror traders +1.15; mirror traders -10.98; mirrored trades -61.24; self-initiated trades of mirror traders -6.88. Correlation between 30-day performance and popularity 0.11; mirroring choice log-odds coefficient on popularity 15.68 vs 0.34 on performance; "explorers" who frequently revise whom they copy outperform static copiers (slope 2.06 in data); a 10% reduction in the popularity-to-performance weighting raises overall performance 6.6% in the model — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.01817v2); [arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.01817v2)
- Followme 2025 Mid-Year Trading Report (published 2025-07-17): 17,727 active accounts; copy-trading accounts 47.27% profitable with average net gain USD 322; self-directed accounts averaged USD -3,236; signal providers averaged USD -1,297; losing accounts outnumber winners 2.1 to 1; XAU/USD was 85.7% of platform volume with USD 15.5M in losses across 14,817 accounts (down from USD 38.6M) — [Finance Magnates (sponsored)](https://www.financemagnates.com/thought-leadership/followmes-2025-mid-year-report-reveals-global-retail-trends/)
- "YieldFund Multi-Exchange Study (2025)" as reported by KuCoin: >100,000 copy-trading outcomes on Binance, Bybit and MEXC over 90 days; 48.48% of followers profitable; 43.61% of lead traders delivered positive follower returns while 97% had positive personal PnL; Binance follower win rate 66.50%, Bybit 43.65%, MEXC 57.79% with net follower PnL -210,040 USDT — [KuCoin blog (competitor-published; primary study not verified)](https://www.kucoin.com/blog/is-crypto-copytrading-profitable-in-2026)
- DWF Ventures study of Fomo social-trading app (reported 2026-08-28): 292,000 wallets over three months; 6.16% profitable on realized gains; 25 wallets cleared $10,000 net profit; losses attributed to callers selling into follower inflows — [Yellow.com](https://yellow.com/news/fomo-copy-trading-94-percent-wallet-losses)
- MQL5 Market audit (Oct 2026): all paid copy signals linked to the 10 best-selling scalping/martingale EAs had zero subscribers; 6 of 10 products had reviews alleging losses or blown accounts — [MQL5 blog 777027](https://www.mql5.com/en/blogs/post/777027)
- MQL5 subscriber reviews on long-lived scalping signals report losses while the master gained (Scalp IC ECN: "9 orders earned $75 for the signal while they lost $10"; HJM1: "Lost 7% in the trial month with real money") — [Scalp IC Markets ECN](https://www.mql5.com/en/signals/683917); [HJM1](https://www.mql5.com/en/signals/2343251)
- Earlier academic context: Berger, Wenzel & Wohlgemuth (2018) analyzed 16,964 eToro observations and found experience plus imitation combined with low risk explain performance; a University of Vienna thesis concluded eToro and Wikifolio cannot consistently be said to outperform the S&P 500 (full text returned HTTP 401) — [Leuphana publication record](https://fis.leuphana.de/en/publications/imitation-related-performance-outcomes-in-social-trading-a-config/); [Univie thesis (blocked)](https://utheses-gateway.univie.ac.at/api/document/get/file/73534)
- Traders Union survey (date not captured; page returned 403): 46% of users started copy trading expecting passive income; 36% chose traders mainly on historical profitability — [Traders Union (search snippet)](https://tradersunion.com/news/business-wealth/show/2493264-traders-union-research-copy-trading/)
- Regulatory baseline cited by an aggregator: ESMA-related disclosures show 74-89% of retail CFD accounts lose money — [CoinLaw](https://coinlaw.io/ftmo-statistics/)

### Inferences
- The eToro finding that mirrored trades lose ~61 bps on average while the same traders' own trades lose ~7 bps is the clearest quantitative evidence that copying itself (selection by popularity plus execution lag) destroys value; this maps directly onto MQL5 scalping signals where the per-trade edge is 1-3 pips and copier slippage is 1-2+ pips.
- Across forex (Followme 47%), crypto exchanges (48%) and on-chain social trading (6%), the share of profitable followers never exceeds half in independent data, while platform-run reports (Bitget 2023: 82-93% profitable) are outliers.
- No study specifically isolates scalping-signal subscribers; the MQL5 audit's zero-subscriber finding and the slippage complaints are the only subscriber-level evidence for scalping.

### Gaps
- The YieldFund primary study and the "12,480 mirrored trades from 27 providers" slippage audit (influencerq-web.pages.dev, host not resolvable from this session) could not be read; the latter's findings are unknown.
- No 2025-2026 academic paper on MQL5 Signals, Myfxbook AutoTrade or Darwinex follower returns was found; the only academic work located uses 2010-2013 eToro data.
