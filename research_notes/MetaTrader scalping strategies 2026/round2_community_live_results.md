## Key Question 1: What do real traders report earning or losing scalping in 2024-2026, in numbers? Which methods/instruments/brokers recur in positive vs negative reports?

### Takeaway
Across ~50 first-hand reports with figures (all from MQL5 buyer pages, MQL5 blogs, Trustpilot and one forum thread), the positive reports are overwhelmingly early-life (first 1-12 weeks after purchase or EA launch), small (+3.5% to +20% over 2-12 weeks on $1k-$10k accounts) and concentrated on grid/recovery EAs (Quantum Emperor on GBPUSD H1) and one AUDNZD limit-order night scalper (NoCap, on Fusion Markets); the negative reports are account wipe-outs or 15-100% losses that cluster on four dates (Feb-Mar 2024 and 17-21 Jun 2024 for Quantum Emperor; Oct 2025 for gold grids; 1-7 Oct 2026 for Quantum Queen X / Athena X gold grids) plus a news-spike stop-out on the night scalper (AUD CPI, Oct 2026). No first-hand 2024-2026 report of a *manual* MT4/MT5 scalper with a verifiable multi-month P&L was found on any reachable source.

### Inferences
- Positive reports are dominated by early-life observations and by users who later received free licences or giveaway prizes (Cociu, TradeSmart13, Ferran Lopez Navarro), so the positive:negative ratio on vendor pages overstates survivorship.
- The two Puscasu signals that are reachable both started in Sep 2025 on $100-$250 initial deposits with deposits of 14x-108x the initial balance and withdrawals nearly equal to deposits; their "growth" percentages (220%, 412%) are time-weighted and are not comparable to buyer account outcomes.
- No reachable first-hand report describes a manual scalper making verifiable multi-month profits in 2024-2026; the only manual journals found are two days of sub-$30 trades by a tool vendor.

### Gaps
- Reddit, Medium, Trade2Win, Forex Factory, FPA, Myfxbook, BabyPips are all unreadable; Elite Trader, NexusFi, QuantConnect, TradingView, Quora, YouTube and Finance Magnates/FX News Group returned no relevant results in the 10 searches available, so no non-MQL5 manual-scalping journals with numbers were obtained.
- No prop-firm payout statements for scalpers were found first-hand; the single prop-firm story (Medium) is a search snippet.
- Real/demo status is not displayed on the Puscasu signal pages read; the QE page even labels them "demonstration signals".

## Key Question 2: For each EA blow-up, what happened, when, how much did users lose, what was the mechanism, and how did the vendor respond?

### Takeaway
Every documented blow-up is a grid/averaging/recovery system meeting a one-directional move: Quantum Emperor (GBPUSD recovery multiplier 1.8, up to ~50 positions) in Feb-Mar and 17-21 Jun 2024 (user losses $1,000-$2,600 on $1k-$3k accounts, one independent reviewer put the signal's June drawdown at 70%); Quantum Queen X / Athena X gold grids in the 1-7 Oct 2026 gold move (eight named buyers report €700-$3,000 accounts going to ~zero within days of a 14/23 Jul 2026 launch); Waka Waka NZDCAD/AUDNZD grids in 2023-2024 (40-50% DD, blown accounts); a NoCap night scalper's 60-pip stop filled ~168 pips away on AUD CPI (Oct 2026). ## Key Question 3: Which scalpers do community veterans point to as genuinely profitable over years, and what evidence do they offer?

### Takeaway
No veteran on any reachable source names a retail MT4/MT5 scalper or scalping EA as verifiably profitable over multiple years; the strongest evidence offered for "genuinely profitable" is the vendor's own multi-year signal (Night Hunter Pro, 315 weeks, +139.72%, PF 1.22) whose recent monthly growth is negative, and an October 2026 audit that found every profitable scalping signal ran on a $50-300 account at 1:500-1:1000 for 4-54 weeks with zero paying subscribers. What veterans do agree on is a design profile: one position at a time, no recovery/stacking, ATR-based stops and targets, hard daily loss limits, withdraw capital once doubled, and treat a "95% win rate with largest loss >10x average win" as the fingerprint of hidden risk.
